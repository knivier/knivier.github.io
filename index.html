About Me

Hi, I'm Agniva Ganguly (Knivier)!

I'm a University of Michigan student in Ann Arbor, targeting a Computer Engineering BSE at the College of Engineering. Online I go by Knivier. I've always been drawn to how computers work from the ground up - hardware, software, and everything in between.

Day to day I dig into Java / Rust / C, embedded systems, and OS development. When I'm not coding, you'll find me playing tennis, Minecraft, or hiking.

Note: Not all of my projects are on here or GitHub - some university and research work stays private. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to hear more about what I do.
